# NutEV Scientific Benchmark — Pre-results Amendments

## Amendment 1 — primary judgment pool systems

Status: **PRE-RESULTS / NO HUMAN LABELS OBSERVED**  
Recorded: **2026-08-18**  
Frozen NutEV runtime remains: `6aa7a5fe6009776e611ca3e1506486606b05f4f6`

### Change

The primary human judgment pool for `COMMON_POOL_PRIORITIZATION` is defined as the union, per question, of:

- top 100 from `nutev_full`; and
- top 100 from `lexical_baseline`.

The six NutEV ablations, `recency_baseline` and `union_unranked` remain generated by the label-blind ranking harness, but they do **not** enlarge the primary judgment pool by default.

### Reason

The preregistered primary scientific comparison is `nutev_full` versus `lexical_baseline`. Including the top 100 from every secondary/ablation system in the first human pool could multiply assessor workload without being necessary to compute the primary endpoint or its recall guard.

Restricting the default primary pool to the candidate and primary baseline therefore aligns the judged universe with the primary claim and makes complete double assessment more feasible.

### Safeguards

- This amendment was recorded before any human relevance labels were observed or generated.
- The frozen NutEV runtime, score, taxonomy, queries and ranking weights are unchanged.
- Pool depth remains 100 per selected system.
- The pool remains deterministically shuffled and system/rank membership remains hidden from assessors.
- Secondary or ablation claims requiring documents outside the primary pool need an explicitly declared supplemental judgment pool; they may not silently reuse unjudged items as relevance 0.
- The primary result may not be redefined after labels are observed.

### Scientific boundary

This amendment changes only the **human judgment workload/universe for the primary comparison**. It does not supply evidence, improve measured performance, or change the current verdict of **B — DEMOTE**.
